Medical Preprocessing Pipeline

1. Skull Stripping (Extreme Contour Cropping): Automatically isolates cerebral brain tissue from cranial bones and background margins using Otsu adaptive thresholding and morphological closing.

2. CLAHE Contrast Enhancement: Operates on the luminance channel of the LAB color space to enhance subtle lesion boundaries and micro-structures without amplifying background noise.

3. Bilateral Edge-Preserving Denoising: Smooths MRI acquisition grain while keeping pathological tumor margins sharp.

Deep Learning & Explainable AI (XAI)

1. Multi-Model Vision Backbones: Evaluated DenseNet-121, ResNet-50, EfficientNet-B0, and custom CBAM Attention CNN trained on the Kaggle 7,023 brain MRI dataset.

2. Grad-CAM++ Saliency Mapping: Uses higher-order gradient weighting to localize complex, diffused, or micro-scale brain tumors with pixel-level precision.

3. Clinical Quantitative Metrics: Computes automated bounding boxes, center-of-mass centroids, estimated tumor burden percentage, and anatomical quadrant localization.
